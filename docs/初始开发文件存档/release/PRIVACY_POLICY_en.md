# Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 — effective 2026-08-12

The app requires no online account. Ledgers, attachments, settings, and operation records stay on the device by default and are protected by SQLCipher, Android Keystore, and authenticated encryption. The card Vault has a separate key hierarchy, and each sensitive reveal, copy, or edit requires fresh device authentication.

Offline recording does not depend on a network. The app connects only when the user explicitly invokes map styles, an exchange-rate service that receives only a currency pair and date, encrypted Google Drive backup within the `drive.file` scope, or a separately consented diagnostics receiver. The app has no ads, does not sell data, and never sends amounts, accounts, cards, notes, merchants, places, attachments, or backup contents.

Feature telemetry and crash reporting are separate and off by default. Nothing is collected or sent before policy consent. If enabled, only fixed enums and fixed schemas are eligible. The installation ID rotates every 30 days; feature events are retained for at most 90 days and crash reports for at most 180 days. Users can inspect or delete local queues. Disabling either option deletes its queue and installation identifier.

Notification, document-directory, location, biometric, and Drive permissions are requested only for the related feature. Ordinary exports never include a full card number, security code, or Vault field; coordinates are off by default. Backups are always encrypted. User-controlled external exports and Drive backups are outside Clear this device; cloud deletion has a separate reauthentication flow.

Users may withdraw diagnostics consent, clear device data, separately delete cloud backups, or uninstall the app. Recovery passwords cannot be recovered, and there is no application or server bypass. Before Play publication, the publisher must provide the final policy URL and support contact in the store listing; publication must not proceed while either is absent.
